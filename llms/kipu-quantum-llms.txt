# Kipu Quantum

> Kipu Quantum GmbH is a Berlin-based deep-tech company building application-specific, hardware-efficient quantum algorithms and delivering them through the Kipu Quantum Hub — a managed cloud platform for industrial quantum computing with unified access to 20+ quantum backends, a visual workflow designer, an AI matchmaker (Tinkuq), and a Python SDK / REST API / CLI.

## Company
- [Website](https://kipu-quantum.com): Company homepage.
- [Kipu Quantum Hub](https://hub.kipu-quantum.com): End-to-end platform for industrial quantum computing.
- [Platform / SDK](https://kipu-quantum.com/platform): Product overview, SDK and REST API access.
- [Pricing](https://kipu-quantum.com/pricing): Free, Plus, Team, and Enterprise plans.
- [Blog](https://kipu-quantum.com/blog): Technical articles on quantum optimization, ML, and hardware co-design.
- [Examples](https://kipu-quantum.com/examples): Application demonstrations (optimization, routing, classification).
- [Imprint](https://kipu-quantum.com/imprint): Legal entity — Kipu Quantum GmbH, Berlin, Germany.
